The problem
A college loses touch with each batch at convocation, and the usual fixes do not hold it.
Alumni drift.
After convocation the college keeps a roll number and an old email address. Nothing brings the graduate back.
Final-years need referrals.
The people best placed to give them are recent graduates the college can no longer reach.
Hosted platforms lapse.
When the subscription ends, the member list stays with the vendor unless someone exported it in time.
An open directory is a liability.
A public list of names, batches and employers is easy to scrape. Under the DPDP Act, the college answers for it.

Who it is for
Each group on campus gets the part of the network its job needs, and no more.
Alumni
Find batchmates by batch, branch, company or city. Decide who sees your email and phone. Post jobs, mentor, and register for reunions.
Final-year students
Search alumni by branch, batch and company. Ask for mentoring or a referral. Apply to jobs that alumni post.
Faculty, HODs and the placement cell
HODs review registrations from their own department only, and run department events. Faculty can search both cohorts. The placement cell approves every job before students see it, and CGPA stays on the placement side.
Alumni cell
Works the verification queue, runs events and check-in, publishes news and galleries, and writes to batches.
Nine roles, one permission table. One person can hold several roles and gets exactly what each allows.

09
Privacy and DPDP
The privacy rules are enforced on the server, and ifBash
cannot search or export your members.
Between members
Nobody searches your alumni without signing in. There is no public list.
Contact details need two yeses: the viewer must be entitled, and the owner’s own setting must allow it. The one exception is your college administrator.
A student sees an alumnus’s email or phone only after that alumnus accepts the student’s request.
CGPA and placement status stay with the placement cell. Students never see giving.
Between your college and us
Only your college administrator can export contacts in bulk or act on DPDP requests. An export needs a written reason for the audit log. A request starts a 30-day clock.
ifBash’s admin account sets up your college and its branding. It cannot open a profile, search the directory, export contacts or see a data request.
It can read the audit log. An entry keeps the record the action changed, so it shows member details such as names and roll numbers.
The portal is designed to keep data in India (AWS Mumbai). Hosting is not live yet.

The trust’s FCRA registration status, before any giving is switched on
