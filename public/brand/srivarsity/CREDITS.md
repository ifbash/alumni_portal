# Demo media — Tamarind Grove University (`srivarsity`)

illustrate what a college's own media looks like in the portal; they do not
show any real college's campus, staff or students as that college, and the
people in them have not endorsed the product. Keep them out of anything
labelled as a real customer (DIET or otherwise).
